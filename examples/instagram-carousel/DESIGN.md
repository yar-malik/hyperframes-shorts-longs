# Design system

## Canvas

- Size: **1080×1350 px** (Instagram portrait, 4:5).
- Safe margin: approximately **50 px** on every side.
- Output: editable SVG plus upload-ready PNG.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy ink | `#07111F` | Dark backgrounds, primary text, panels. |
| Warm paper | `#F4F0E7` | Light editorial backgrounds. |
| White | `#FFFDF8` | Text and paper cards on dark slides. |
| Signal orange | `#FF5A1F` | Contrarian words, failures, arrows, calls to action. |
| System blue | `#1877F2` | Structure, passes, checks, system cues. |
| Cyan | `#62D6FF` | Small technical labels on dark backgrounds. |
| Cool grey | `#AAB6C5` | Supporting copy on dark slides. |

## Typography

The generator uses Arial/Helvetica-compatible system fonts to stay portable.

- Cover and key headlines: 72–92 px, weight 900.
- Section headlines: 76–88 px, weight 900.
- Supporting copy: 27–29 px, weight 700.
- Technical labels: 15–19 px, weight 900, increased letter spacing.
- Keep headlines short enough to read on a phone without zooming.

## Composition rules

- Alternate dark and light environments to create swipe rhythm.
- Give each slide one dominant idea and one dominant visual.
- Use orange for emphasis sparingly; a single phrase is stronger than a full orange paragraph.
- Keep the masthead, page number, domain footer, and swipe cue consistent.
- Use photos on 2–3 slides, not every slide.
- Avoid repeating the same rounded-card grid on consecutive pages.
- Add tactile cues—paper, tape, grain, annotations—without reducing legibility.

## Personalization rule

The avatar is not decoration. Each use should support the narrative:

- The creator owns the workflow on the cover.
- The creator embodies the self-review problem on slide 2.
- The creator signs off on the takeaway on slide 8.

## Quality gate

- All eight slides are 1080×1350.
- No text touches the 50 px outer safe area.
- Body copy is at least 27 px.
- Orange text has enough contrast against its background.
- The portrait crop does not cut through eyes, chin, or hairline.
- The cover remains understandable as a small profile-grid thumbnail.
