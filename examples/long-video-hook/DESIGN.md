# Design direction

Concept angle: a live motion-design command deck turns three ordinary inputs into a complete animated system while the presenter remains visible as the guide.

- Canvas: 1920×1080 landscape.
- Layout: presenter full-screen for 0–3 s, then a rounded canvas window on the left (1536×1080 scaled to 90%) and a floating 340×560 presenter card on the right.
- Palette: warm paper `#f3eee4`, ink `#11100e`, signal orange `#d94c23`, slate blue `#274761`.
- Type: Archivo Black for display copy; Space Mono for labels and metadata.
- Focal elements: the changing left-side diagram and the presenter’s face.
- Edge anchors: top progress rail, orange offset shadow on the presenter card, bottom caption band.
- Background: paper grid, localized orange glow, and subtle grain.
- Motion: slow push-in on the full-screen presenter, a 0.75 s shrink into the side card at 3 s, varied directional entrances, continuous but bounded canvas motion, and hard topic cuts at sentence boundaries.
